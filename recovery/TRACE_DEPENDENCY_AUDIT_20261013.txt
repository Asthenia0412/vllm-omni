6. Guard/runtime boundaries

Cross BSND/combined was disabled in this traced variant, so this trace cannot attribute any cost to that historical combined guard. QK and recv host custom-op ranges include their actual C++ admission and dispatch work; Python provider/runtime checks may also lie in caller intervals outside those ranges. Inspected descriptor checks read host-side tensor/storage metadata and runtime stream/capture state; no explicit device synchronization appears in those checks. They cannot be declared free, and the trace does not separate their exclusive CPU cost. Existing metadata-copy synchronization waits include pending device work; owner/descriptor checking time must not be inferred by subtracting kernel duration from a host range or by summing waits.

The actionable restoration order remains original QK single-host scheduling, actual cross BSND plus combined guard, and original-equivalent recv/VAE work. Re-profile the restored combination for remaining dependency delays. This audit establishes concrete dependencies and a missing historical environment proof; it does not establish new-feature performance gains or justify weakening correctness/lifetime checks.
