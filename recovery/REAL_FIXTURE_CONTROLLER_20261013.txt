READERS AND PROOF BOUNDARIES
check_real_fixtures.py requires actual complete six-history/four-probe coverage
on every rank, all40 descriptors,192 layer0 cases per role, exact operand names,
actual backend receipts, owner-map bijection, schema/source/build agreement and
full-owner hashes. Reused payload paths have their cached actual SHA compared
against EVERY declaration. Empty operand maps and split owner aliases fail.
Missing histories cannot be filled by duplicates, chunk-number inference or
synthetic inputs. A valid matrix still does not admit a candidate kernel/model.

load_real_fixtures.py imports only stdlib until a caller explicitly materializes
CPU/device tensors. Public API:
  case_paths(root, kind, rank, require_complete=True) ->24 actual layer0 JSONs
  loaded = load_case(path) -> full byte-owner hash/layout/alias validation
  replay = loaded.materialize(torch, device, describe, allocate_owner)
For CPU use device='cpu' without callbacks. Device callers must provide the
original11field descriptor function and an explicit-format allocator callback:
  allocate_owner(base_sizes,dtype,device,npu_format)
For example, a root-authorized device harness may supply torch_npu.empty_with_format
with acl_format=npu_format. The loader never changes storage format labels.
It validates actual device, full physical extent, base/view shape/stride/offset,
pointer relation and distinct owner identity before copying complete owners.
It returns inputs, outputs, owners and host_owners; callers retain them through
consumer completion. Inputs/output reference snapshots stay independent, while
aliases within each snapshot share their original full owner.

QK inputs include pre-RMS raw QKV, both norm gammas and original plus expanded
cosine/sine; output.native_send is the actual original packed result. Metadata
records the real shared coefficient generation. Replaying layer0 forty times
is not all40 real payload coverage. Benchmark reports must distinguish a single
producer.prepare per actual forward from one producer per leaf, and cannot
claim complete model throughput from isolated leaf replays.
